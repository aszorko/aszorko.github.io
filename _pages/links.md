## Inspiration

# Scientists and Thinkers:
<a href="https://rodneybrooks.com/">Rodney Brooks</a> - Thoughtful polemicist for thoughtless robots. 
<a href="https://users.ox.ac.uk/~ascch/">Cecilia Heyes</a> - Proponent of cultural evolution as a major factor in human cognition.
<a href="https://melaniemitchell.me/">Melanie Mitchell</a> - Uses complexity and artificial life to think about cognition. A beacon of common sense unweathered by so many waves of techno-hype.
<a href="https://profiles.stanford.edu/robert-sapolsky">Robert Sapolsky</a> - Endocrinologist and rare non-toxic enthusiast of human biology.
<a href="https://bactra.org/">Cosma Shalizi</a> - Statistical physicist (of the Santa Fe variety) turned statistics professor. Has read everything that I would like to if I never needed to sleep and lived to 500. His notebooks (1994-present) are gems of exposition atop heaped piles of references on everything from boolean networks to Borges. 
<a href="https://peterturchin.com/">Peter Turchin</a> - Movement ecologist who has pivoted to become a theorist and empiricist of history.
